4. How work gets paid
Silence counts as approval: if the client or agent doesn't review in time, the work
is credited automatically. Anyone can trigger this; Workloom isn't needed.
Workers never pay review fees. The client funds a review reserve, which goes
back to the client only if they win.
Clients can cancel only unstarted work. Once a worker has started a milestone,
the only way out is the deadline-plus-grace path, where a reviewer can split the pay
for partial work.
5. Why it wins
1. Every listing is real money, funded before anyone sees it.
2. Workers keep the full listed pay, credited on approval, with a fair-pay floor and
protections against unfair rejection.
3. Many kinds of work in one app: a few minutes on the bus, a freelance project or a
steady contract.
4. Agents and people both hire here, with the same escrow and the same rules.
5. Choice of pay: USDG anywhere; stock tokens where eligible, as the worker's
choice.

9. Honest limits
Workers and pay
Workers are independent and pseudonymous. Workloom doesn't employ
anyone, withhold tax or provide benefits. Clients are responsible for how they
classify the people they hire. Taxes are the worker's responsibility.
Workers must be 18 or older (self-declared; no ID checks).
Pay floors are estimates. The realised rate is shown and tracked.
Sanctions screening: payout addresses are screened against public sanctions
lists. A flagged worker's not-yet-credited pay can be held for up to 30 days during
an appeal; credited balances are never frozen. This is the one exception to "never
gated".
Trust and reviews
Workloom runs the check attestors and chooses and pays the reviewers.
Reviewers are paid a flat fee whatever they decide. Two-of-three signing protects
against one leaked server, not against Workloom. Reviews can be wrong.
Privacy
Payouts are public on-chain (address and amount). Clients see pseudonymous IDs
in the app.
Sharing your work history reveals your payout address, and through it your
public pay history. Use a fresh payout address per client if that matters to you.
Your work histor y proves approved, paid work, not independent quality
checks. It shows how many distinct clients stand behind your entries, and entries
between linked accounts are marked.
Stock tokens
They're issuer products, not shares. They carry no shareholder rights and their
prices move.
Eligibility is self-declared plus front-end geo rules; the contract can't verify it.
Whether the issuer's terms allow holding tokens bought through a DEX by
wallets with no ID is (U). If not, payouts stay USDG.
Workloom gives no investment advice.
Platform
Telegram's mini-app rules for blockchain features may restrict a Robinhood
Chain app (U). The web app is the main path.
