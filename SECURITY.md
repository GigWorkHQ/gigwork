# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **gigwork@atomicmail.io** with the subject `SECURITY`. Include the affected endpoint or file, steps to reproduce, and the impact you expect. Please do not open a public issue, and do not test against other people's funds or accounts.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours and to share a fix plan within 7 days. If you report a confirmed issue in good faith, we will credit you in the audit history unless you prefer otherwise.

**In scope:** the API in `backend/`, the live web app at www.gigworkhq.com, and anything that moves or locks USDG.
**Out of scope:** the sample-data preview pages, denial of service by sheer traffic volume, and social engineering.

## Roles and powers

| Role | Who | Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Client | Any signed-in wallet | Post and fund work; approve, reject or request changes; cancel unstarted work; recover USDG its escrows hold beyond what they owe | Touch another client's escrow, or recover money that backs open work |
| Worker | Any signed-in wallet | Claim, submit and dispute; set a pay handle and payout choice; share history | Pay themselves; claim unfunded work |
| Agent API key | Minted by a wallet session | Act as its owner on work routes, e.g. post a batch or read submissions | Manage keys, claim bonuses, recover funds, or use any team route |
| Reviewer | Wallets the team adds | Decide disputes they are randomly drawn for, before the deadline | Decide cases they are a party to, or that aren't assigned to them |
| Team wallet | `TEAM_WALLET_ADDRESSES`, with a wallet session only | Rule on disputes as backstop (pay one party or the other, or split where allowed); add or remove reviewers; approve or reject referral bonuses; retry a failed payout to its original recipient | Send escrow funds to anyone other than the two parties to a case; change a payout's recipient or amount |
| Keeper | The API process, every 30 s | Default approvals, expiry refunds, Pay runs, reviewer draws, payout retries, ops alerts | Anything a route wouldn't allow; every step is idempotent |
| Operator | Whoever holds the production env and database | **Everything.** See accepted risks below. | |

Every team action that changes who is paid or who reviews is written to the `team_actions` log (`GET /v1/team/actions`).

## Accepted risks

These are known and deliberate for now. We state them so nobody has to discover them.

1. **Custodial escrow.** Escrow wallets are generated by the API, with keys encrypted under a single `DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY` and stored in the same database. An attacker with both could move every escrowed balance. There is no KMS, multisig or on-chain timelock yet. Keep escrowed totals modest until escrow moves to audited contracts (see the roadmap in [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#roadmap)).
2. **Single-key team wallets.** Team powers are bounded (see above), but each team wallet is a single key, not a Safe.
3. **Shared gas wallet.** One hot wallet pays gas for every payout. Abuse is limited by a minimum amount per listing, a daily cap on new listings per client, and per-IP rate limits. The keeper alerts when the wallet runs low (`/health/ops`, `GAS_ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL`). An empty gas wallet delays payouts but loses no funds.
4. **Trusted RPC.** Deposit verification and balances come from one RPC endpoint. A lying RPC could make the API record a deposit that never happened.
5. **Token issuer controls.** USDG's issuer can freeze addresses. A payout to a frozen address fails, waits in the failed-payout queue, and is retried by the team once the cause is fixed.
6. **Team override.** The team can rule on any open review case at any time, including one already assigned to a reviewer. This is the backstop when reviewers are absent or compromised. It is logged.

## Supply chain

Dependencies are pinned to exact versions with a frozen lockfile. CI runs an OSV advisory check, CodeQL and secret scanning on every push. GitHub Actions are pinned to commit SHAs.
